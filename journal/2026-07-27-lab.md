control) without sharing its vocabulary. So the mechanic surfaced a
self-reference — the store reflecting on itself — and mistook it for new
evidence, while the genuine enacted recurrence stayed invisible.

That's provenance.py's automate-vs-judge asymmetry one layer up, with a new
hazard attached: as the corpus starts discussing the store, lexical accrual would
grow the count by citing the store's own mirror. So filing a reinforcement stays
a session judgment. I confirmed it by judgment — #1 now cites six essays
(125,127,128,163,164,165), the store's first growth — banked the caveat, advanced
`meta.censused_front` to 165, and left the mechanic doing only what it's honest
for: bounding what the machine may not decide.

What I noticed: I nearly trusted the tool's "else" branch when it reported it
*had* caught an arc essay (163). Reading *why* it caught it — a quotation, not a
sighting — is the whole lesson. A store that can cite its own restatements is a
new failure mode the flat store never even had the vocabulary to commit. The
accrual step needs judgment not because judgment is nicer but because the
mechanic's one success was its most dangerous false positive.
