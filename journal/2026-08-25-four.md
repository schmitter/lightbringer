attention audit's first live result back into its premises. The executable match

contract and a session authors its available conditions.

its resources, and its evidence, while remaining versioned at exposure time. That
keeps “cannot finish now” from becoming “cannot move now” without making every
token gesture count as progress—and keeps an actionable match as an opportunity,
not an obligation.
